# Full-Load Voltage Regulation, Worked Example

## Problem

The primary and secondary windings of a 40 kVA, 6600/250 V single-phase transformer have resistances of \(10\,\Omega\) and \(0.02\,\Omega\), respectively. The total leakage reactance is \(35\,\Omega\), referred to the primary winding. Find the full-load voltage regulation at 0.8 lagging power factor.

## Solution

Given:

\[
S=40\,\text{kVA}, \quad V_1=6600\,\text{V}, \quad V_2=250\,\text{V}
\]

\[
R_1=10\,\Omega, \quad R_2=0.02\,\Omega, \quad X_{eq1}=35\,\Omega
\]

\[
\cos\phi_2=0.8, \qquad \sin\phi_2=0.6
\]

Refer primary resistance and total reactance to the secondary side:

\[
R_1'=R_1\left(\frac{V_2}{V_1}\right)^2
=10\left(\frac{250}{6600}\right)^2
=0.01435\,\Omega
\]

\[
R_{eq2}=R_2+R_1'=0.02+0.01435=0.03435\,\Omega
\]

\[
X_{eq2}=X_{eq1}\left(\frac{V_2}{V_1}\right)^2
=35\left(\frac{250}{6600}\right)^2
=0.05022\,\Omega
\]

Full-load secondary current:

\[
I_2=\frac{40\times10^3}{250}=160\,\text{A}
\]

For a lagging power factor:

\[
E_2-V_2\approx I_2R_{eq2}\cos\phi_2+I_2X_{eq2}\sin\phi_2
\]

\[
=160(0.03435)(0.8)+160(0.05022)(0.6)
=9.22\,\text{V}
\]

\[
\%\text{VR}=\frac{9.22}{250}\times100=3.69\%
\]
