# DC Shunt-Motor Applications and Series-Motor Characteristics

## Shunt DC motor application

A shunt DC motor is used where speed should remain almost constant, for example in machine tools such as cutting tools.

For approximately constant flux:

\[
T\propto I_a
\]

```text
Torque T
  │    /
  │   /
  │  /
  └──────── Armature current Iₐ
```

## DC series motor

In a series motor, the field winding and armature are in series:

\[
I_L=I_a=I_f
\]

The back emf is:

\[
E_b=V-I_a(R_a+R_{se})
\]

\[
E_b=\frac{\Phi ZPN}{60A}
\]

Therefore:

\[
N\propto\frac{E_b}{\Phi}
\]

Before magnetic saturation, \(\Phi\propto I_a\), hence approximately:

\[
N\propto\frac{E_b}{I_a}
\]

```text
Speed N
  │\
  │ \
  │  \____
  └──────── Armature current Iₐ
```

A DC series motor must not be operated at no load. The small armature current produces a small flux, causing dangerously high speed. This can damage the armature through mechanical and centrifugal stresses.

Torque is:

\[
T\propto\Phi I_a
\]

Before saturation:

\[
T\propto I_a^2
\]

After saturation, flux rises only slightly, so torque becomes approximately proportional to \(I_a\).
