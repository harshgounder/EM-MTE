# Transformer Equivalent Circuit, Worked Example

## Approximate equivalent circuit

For a loaded transformer, the no-load current \(I_0\) is usually about 1% to 3% of rated primary current. It may therefore be neglected for an approximate equivalent circuit without serious error.

### Circuit, secondary quantities referred to the primary side

```text
      R₁        jX₁        R₂′       jX₂′
 V₁ ──/\/\/─────LLLL──────/\/\/──────LLLL────── Z₂′
  │                                                    │
  └────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

The prime (′) denotes a secondary quantity referred to the primary side.

## Problem

A 30 kVA, 2000/200 V, single-phase, 50 Hz transformer has:

| Quantity | Primary | Secondary |
|---|---:|---:|
| Resistance | \(R_1=3.5\,\Omega\) | \(R_2=0.015\,\Omega\) |
| Leakage reactance | \(X_1=4.5\,\Omega\) | \(X_2=0.02\,\Omega\) |

Find the equivalent resistance, reactance, and impedance referred to the primary side.

## Solution

The turns ratio is:

\[
a=\frac{N_1}{N_2}=\frac{V_1}{V_2}=\frac{2000}{200}=10
\]

Refer secondary resistance and reactance to the primary:

\[
R_2'=R_2a^2=0.015(10)^2=1.5\,\Omega
\]

\[
X_2'=X_2a^2=0.02(10)^2=2\,\Omega
\]

Therefore:

\[
R_{eq}=R_1+R_2'=3.5+1.5=5\,\Omega
\]

\[
X_{eq}=X_1+X_2'=4.5+2=6.5\,\Omega
\]

\[
Z_{eq}=R_{eq}+jX_{eq}=5+j6.5\,\Omega
\]

\[
|Z_{eq}|=\sqrt{5^2+6.5^2}=8.20\,\Omega
\]
