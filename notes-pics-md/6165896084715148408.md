# DC Shunt Generator Power Developed and Armature Reaction

## Problem

A 20 kW, 200 V shunt generator has armature resistance \(R_a=0.05\,\Omega\) and shunt-field resistance \(R_f=200\,\Omega\). Calculate the power developed in the armature when it delivers rated output.

## Solution

Rated load current:

\[
I_L=\frac{20\times10^3}{200}=100\,\text{A}
\]

Shunt-field current:

\[
I_f=\frac{V_t}{R_f}=\frac{200}{200}=1\,\text{A}
\]

For a shunt generator:

\[
I_a=I_L+I_f=101\,\text{A}
\]

Generated emf:

\[
E_g=V_t+I_aR_a
=200+101(0.05)
=205.05\,\text{V}
\]

Power developed in the armature:

\[
P_{dev}=E_gI_a
=205.05(101)
=20.71\,\text{kW}
\]

## Armature reaction

Armature reaction is the effect of armature flux on the main field produced by the field winding. It distorts and weakens the main field flux and therefore affects DC-machine performance. Its components include cross-magnetising and demagnetising effects.

With the usual convention:

- In a DC generator, the magnetic neutral axis shifts in the direction of rotation.
- In a DC motor, it shifts opposite to the direction of rotation.
