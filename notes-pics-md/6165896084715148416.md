# DC Motor Torque Equation and Worked Example

## Torque equation

Mechanical power developed in the armature is:

\[
P_{dev}=E_bI_a
\]

Also:

\[
P_{dev}=T\omega
\]

where:

\[
\omega=2\pi n=2\pi\left(\frac{N}{60}\right)
\]

Thus:

\[
T=\frac{E_bI_a}{2\pi n}
\]

Using \(E_b=\Phi ZPN/(60A)\):

\[
T=\frac{\Phi ZP}{2\pi A}I_a
\]

Therefore:

\[
T\propto\Phi I_a
\]

## Worked problem

Calculate the armature torque developed by a 440 V DC shunt motor with armature resistance \(0.25\,\Omega\), running at 750 rpm and taking armature current \(60\,\text{A}\).

### Solution

\[
E_b=V-I_aR_a
=440-60(0.25)
=425\,\text{V}
\]

\[
\omega=2\pi\left(\frac{750}{60}\right)=78.54\,\text{rad/s}
\]

\[
T=\frac{E_bI_a}{\omega}
=\frac{425(60)}{78.54}
=324.7\,\text{N m}
\]
