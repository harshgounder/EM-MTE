# Transformer Efficiency and Maximum Efficiency

## Efficiency

\[
\eta=\frac{\text{output}}{\text{output}+P_i+P_{cu}}
\]

For secondary-side quantities:

\[
\eta=\frac{V_2I_2\cos\phi_2}{V_2I_2\cos\phi_2+P_i+P_{cu}}
\]

where:

- \(P_i\) is the iron, or core, loss.
- \(P_{cu}\) is the copper loss.
- \(V_2I_2\) is the output kVA at the operating load.

Copper loss can be written as:

\[
P_{cu}=I_2^2R_{eq2}=I_1^2R_{eq1}=I_1^2R_1+I_2^2R_2
\]

## Condition for maximum efficiency

Transformer efficiency is maximum when:

\[
P_{cu}=P_i
\]

Therefore, for secondary-referred quantities:

\[
I_2^2R_{eq2}=P_i
\]

\[
I_2=\sqrt{\frac{P_i}{R_{eq2}}}
\]

This is the secondary current corresponding to maximum efficiency.

If \(P_{cu,FL}\) is full-load copper loss, the load fraction for maximum efficiency is:

\[
x=\sqrt{\frac{P_i}{P_{cu,FL}}}
\]

Hence the output kVA at maximum efficiency is:

\[
S_{max\,\eta}=S_{rated}\sqrt{\frac{P_i}{P_{cu,FL}}}
\]

At a specified power factor \(\cos\phi\):

\[
\eta_{max}=\frac{S_{max\,\eta}\cos\phi}
{S_{max\,\eta}\cos\phi+2P_i}
\]

Multiply by 100 to express \(\eta_{max}\) as a percentage.
