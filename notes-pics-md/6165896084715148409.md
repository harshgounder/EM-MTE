# DC Motors, Shunt Motor Fundamentals

## Types of DC motor

1. Shunt DC motor.
2. Series DC motor.
3. Compound DC motor.

## Shunt DC motor current relations

\[
I_L=I_a+I_f
\]

where \(I_L\) is line current, \(I_a\) is armature current, and \(I_f\) is field current.

\[
I_f=\frac{V}{R_f}
\]

For comparison, in a shunt generator:

\[
I_a=I_L+I_f
\]

## Back emf

For a DC motor:

\[
E_b=V-I_aR_a
\]

For a generator, the corresponding generated emf is:

\[
E_g=V+I_aR_a
\]

The back emf equation is:

\[
E_b=\frac{\Phi ZPN}{60A}
\]

Thus:

\[
E_b\propto\Phi N
\]

## Torque and speed characteristics of a shunt motor

\[
T\propto\Phi I_a
\]

In a shunt motor, field current and flux are approximately constant. Therefore:

\[
T\propto I_a
\]

and, for constant supply voltage:

\[
N\propto\frac{V-I_aR_a}{\Phi}
\]

So speed falls slightly as armature current increases.
