# Transformer Ratings and EMF Equation, Worked Example

## Problem

A 200 kVA, 6600/400 V, 50 Hz single-phase transformer has 80 turns on its secondary winding. Calculate:

1. Approximate primary and secondary currents.
2. Number of primary turns.
3. Maximum value of flux.

## Solution

### 1. Rated currents

For a single-phase transformer, the apparent power is the same on both sides:

\[
V_1I_1=V_2I_2=200\times10^3\ \text{VA}
\]

\[
I_1=\frac{200\times10^3}{6600}=30.30\ \text{A}
\]

\[
I_2=\frac{200\times10^3}{400}=500\ \text{A}
\]

### 2. Primary turns

Neglecting winding drops, \(E_1\approx V_1\) and \(E_2\approx V_2\). Therefore:

\[
\frac{V_1}{V_2}=\frac{N_1}{N_2}
\]

\[
\frac{6600}{400}=\frac{N_1}{80}
\]

\[
N_1=1320\ \text{turns}
\]

### 3. Maximum flux

Use the transformer emf equation:

\[
E_1=4.44fN_1\Phi_m
\]

\[
6600=4.44(50)(1320)\Phi_m
\]

\[
\Phi_m=0.02252\ \text{Wb}
\]

The source answer, \(0.0225\,\text{Wb}\), is correct after rounding.
