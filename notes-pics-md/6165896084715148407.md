\[

## Problem

An 8-pole DC shunt generator has 778 wave-connected armature conductors and runs at 800 rpm. It supplies a 12.5 \(\Omega\) load at a terminal voltage of 250 V. Armature resistance is \(0.24\,\Omega\) and field resistance is \(250\,\Omega\). Find armature current, induced emf, and flux per pole.

### Circuit relationship

```text
             I_L → load R_L = 12.5 Ω
       ┌──────────────┬──────────────┐
       │              │              │
     E_g, R_a       V_t           R_f = 250 Ω
       │              │              │
       └──────────────┴──────────────┘
                       I_f

                     I_a = I_L + I_f
```

### Armature current

\[
I_L=\frac{V_t}{R_L}=\frac{250}{12.5}=20\,\text{A}
\]

\[
I_f=\frac{V_t}{R_f}=\frac{250}{250}=1\,\text{A}
\]

\[
I_a=I_L+I_f=21\,\text{A}
\]

### Induced emf

\[
E_g=V_t+I_aR_a
=250+21(0.24)
=255.04\,\text{V}
\]

### Flux per pole

For a wave winding, \(A=2\). Use:

\[
E_g=\frac{\Phi ZPN}{60A}
\]

\[
\Phi=\frac{E_g(60)A}{ZPN}
=\frac{255.04(60)(2)}{778(8)(800)}
=0.00615\,\text{Wb}
\]

Thus the flux per pole is approximately \(6.15\,\text{mWb}\).

> Check: the source's \(9.8\times10^{-3}\,\text{Wb}\) does not follow from the stated figures.
