# Voltage Regulation from Percentage Drops

## Problem

Calculate the voltage regulation of a transformer for which the ohmic drop is 1% and the reactance drop is 5% of rated voltage, at:

1. 0.8 lagging power factor.
2. 0.8 leading power factor.

## Formula

\[
\%\text{VR}=(\%\text{ohmic drop})\cos\phi\ \pm\ (\%\text{reactance drop})\sin\phi
\]

Use \(+\) for a lagging power factor and \(-\) for a leading power factor.

For \(\cos\phi=0.8\):

\[
\sin\phi=\sqrt{1-0.8^2}=0.6
\]

### 1. 0.8 lagging power factor

\[
\%\text{VR}=1(0.8)+5(0.6)=3.8\%
\]

### 2. 0.8 leading power factor

\[
\%\text{VR}=1(0.8)-5(0.6)=-2.2\%
\]

Negative regulation means the load terminal voltage rises above the no-load voltage.

> Check: the handwritten answers use the unit "volts," but the stated drops and calculation are percentages. The correct units are percent.
