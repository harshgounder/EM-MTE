# Transformer Test Results, Equivalent Circuit, Part 1

## Problem

The following results were obtained for a 50 kVA, 2400/120 V single-phase transformer:

| Test | Test side | Power | Current | Voltage |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Open circuit | Low-voltage | \(396\,\text{W}\) | \(9.65\,\text{A}\) | \(120\,\text{V}\) |
| Short circuit | High-voltage | \(810\,\text{W}\) | \(20.8\,\text{A}\) | \(92\,\text{V}\) |

Find the equivalent-circuit parameters referred to the low-voltage winding.

## Open-circuit test, low-voltage side

Given:

\[
W_0=396\,\text{W}, \qquad V_0=120\,\text{V}, \qquad I_0=9.65\,\text{A}
\]

\[
\cos\theta_0=\frac{W_0}{V_0I_0}=\frac{396}{120(9.65)}=0.342
\]

Core-loss component:

\[
I_w=I_0\cos\theta_0=3.30\,\text{A}
\]

Magnetising component:

\[
I_m=I_0\sqrt{1-\cos^2\theta_0}=9.07\,\text{A}
\]

### Shunt branch referred to the low-voltage side

```text
          I₀
  V₀ ─────┬────────
          │   │
         R₀  jX₀
          │   │
         Iw  Im
          └───┘
```

\[
R_0=\frac{V_0}{I_w}=\frac{120}{3.30}=36.36\,\Omega
\]

\[
X_0=\frac{V_0}{I_m}=\frac{120}{9.07}=13.23\,\Omega
\]
