# Induction-Motor Slip and Rotating-Field Speeds

## Completion of the 960 rpm problem

Given a 50 Hz induction motor with full-load rotor speed \(N_r=960\,\text{rpm}\), the closest lower synchronous speed is:

\[
N_s=1000\,\text{rpm}
\]

The number of poles is:

\[
P=\frac{120f}{N_s}=\frac{120(50)}{1000}=6
\]

Slip is:

\[
s=\frac{N_s-N_r}{N_s}=\frac{1000-960}{1000}=0.04=4\%
\]

Rotor frequency is:

\[
f_2=sf=0.04(50)=2\,\text{Hz}
\]

### Reference-frame speeds

| Quantity | Speed |
|---|---:|
| Stator field relative to stator structure | \(N_s=1000\,\text{rpm}\) |
| Stator field relative to rotor structure | \(N_s-N_r=40\,\text{rpm}\) |
| Rotor field relative to rotor structure | \(N_s-N_r=40\,\text{rpm}\) |
| Rotor field relative to stator structure | \(N_s=1000\,\text{rpm}\) |
| Rotor field relative to stator field | \(0\,\text{rpm}\) |

> Check: the handwritten page assigns 1000 rpm to the rotor field relative to the rotor structure. Relative to the rotor structure it moves at slip speed, 40 rpm. It is 1000 rpm relative to the stator structure.

## Worked problem

A 6-pole induction motor is fed from a 50 Hz supply. Rotor frequency is 2 Hz. Find rotor speed and slip.

\[
N_s=\frac{120(50)}6=1000\,\text{rpm}
\]

\[
s=\frac{f_2}{f}=\frac2{50}=0.04=4\%
\]

\[
N_r=(1-s)N_s=(1-0.04)(1000)=960\,\text{rpm}
\]
