# DC Series-Motor Speed at a New Load

## Problem

A 25 hp, 250 V DC series motor has armature resistance \(R_a=0.1\,\Omega\), series-field resistance \(R_{se}=0.05\,\Omega\), and a 3 V brush drop. When line current is 80 A, speed is 600 rpm. Find the speed when line current is 100 A.

## Solution

For a series motor:

\[
I_L=I_a=I_f
\]

At 80 A:

\[
E_{b1}=V-I_{a1}(R_a+R_{se})-V_{brush}
\]

\[
=250-80(0.1+0.05)-3=235\,\text{V}
\]

At 100 A:

\[
E_{b2}=250-100(0.1+0.05)-3=232\,\text{V}
\]

Before saturation in a series motor, flux is approximately proportional to armature current:

\[
\frac{\Phi_1}{\Phi_2}=\frac{I_{a1}}{I_{a2}}=\frac{80}{100}
\]

Since \(E_b\propto\Phi N\):

\[
N_2=\frac{\Phi_1}{\Phi_2}\frac{E_{b2}}{E_{b1}}N_1
\]

\[
N_2=\frac{80}{100}\frac{232}{235}(600)=473.9\,\text{rpm}
\]

Therefore, the new speed is approximately \(\boxed{474\,\text{rpm}}\).

This calculation assumes the magnetic circuit is unsaturated over the stated current range.
