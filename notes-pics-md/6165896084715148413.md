# DC Shunt-Motor Speed, Worked Examples, Part 1

## Problem 1

A 4-pole, 500 V DC shunt motor has 720 wave-connected armature conductors. At full load, armature current is 60 A and flux per pole is \(0.03\,\text{Wb}\). Armature resistance is \(0.2\,\Omega\), and contact drop is 1 V per brush. Calculate full-load speed.

### Solution

For a wave winding:

\[
A=2
\]

The total brush contact drop is \(2\,\text{V}\), assuming one positive and one negative brush contact, each with a 1 V drop.

\[
E_b=V-I_aR_a-V_{brush}
=500-60(0.2)-2
=486\,\text{V}
\]

Use:

\[
E_b=\frac{\Phi ZPN}{60A}
\]

\[
N=\frac{E_b(60)A}{\Phi ZP}
=\frac{486(60)(2)}{0.03(720)(4)}
=675\,\text{rpm}
\]

> Check: the source gives \(678\,\text{rpm}\) after using \(E_b=488\,\text{V}\). It omits the stated 1 V per-brush contact drops. If contact drop is intentionally neglected, \(678\,\text{rpm}\) is correct.

## Problem 2, started on this page

A 250 V shunt motor runs at 1000 rpm at no load and takes 8 A. Total armature and field resistances are \(0.2\,\Omega\) and \(250\,\Omega\), respectively. Calculate its speed when loaded and taking 50 A, assuming constant flux.

### No-load values

\[
I_f=\frac{250}{250}=1\,\text{A}
\]

\[
I_{a0}=8-1=7\,\text{A}
\]

\[
E_{b0}=250-7(0.2)=248.6\,\text{V}
\]
