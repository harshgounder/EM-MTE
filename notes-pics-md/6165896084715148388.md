# Transformer Copper Loss and Equivalent-Circuit Practice

## Completion of the previous example

Copper loss is \(I^2R\). When all secondary quantities are referred to the primary side, use the primary current and primary-referred equivalent resistance only.

For the 30 kVA, 2000/200 V transformer on the preceding page:

\[
V_1I_1=V_2I_2=30\times10^3\ \text{VA}
\]

\[
I_1=\frac{30\times10^3}{2000}=15\ \text{A}
\]

\[
P_{cu}=I_1^2R_{eq}=15^2(5)=1125\ \text{W}
\]

## Problem

A 50 kVA, 4400/220 V transformer has:

\[
R_1=3.45\,\Omega, \qquad R_2=0.009\,\Omega
\]

\[
X_1=5.2\,\Omega, \qquad X_2=0.015\,\Omega
\]

Calculate:

1. Equivalent resistance and reactance referred to the primary.
2. Equivalent resistance and reactance referred to the secondary.
3. Equivalent impedance referred to each side.
4. Total copper loss at full load.

## Solution

The turns ratio is:

\[
a=\frac{V_1}{V_2}=\frac{4400}{220}=20
\]

### Referred to the primary

\[
R_2'=R_2a^2=0.009(20)^2=3.6\,\Omega
\]

\[
X_2'=X_2a^2=0.015(20)^2=6\,\Omega
\]

\[
R_{eq1}=R_1+R_2'=3.45+3.6=7.05\,\Omega
\]

\[
X_{eq1}=X_1+X_2'=5.2+6=11.2\,\Omega
\]

\[
Z_{eq1}=7.05+j11.2\,\Omega, \qquad |Z_{eq1}|=13.23\,\Omega
\]

### Referred to the secondary

\[
R_1'=\frac{R_1}{a^2}=\frac{3.45}{400}=0.008625\,\Omega
\]

\[
X_1'=\frac{X_1}{a^2}=\frac{5.2}{400}=0.013\,\Omega
\]

\[
R_{eq2}=R_1'+R_2=0.017625\,\Omega
\]

\[
X_{eq2}=X_1'+X_2=0.028\,\Omega
\]

\[
Z_{eq2}=0.017625+j0.028\,\Omega, \qquad |Z_{eq2}|=0.03308\,\Omega
\]

### Full-load copper loss

\[
I_{1,FL}=\frac{50\times10^3}{4400}=11.364\ \text{A}
\]

\[
P_{cu,FL}=I_{1,FL}^2R_{eq1}=11.364^2(7.05)=910.38\ \text{W}
\]

Using the rounded current \(I_1=11.36\,\text{A}\) gives \(909.80\,\text{W}\), the value shown on the following source page.
