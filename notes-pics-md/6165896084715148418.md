# DC Shunt Motor Developed and Shaft Power

## Problem

A 6-pole lap-wound DC shunt motor takes 400 A at 350 rpm. Flux per pole is \(80\,\text{mWb}\), the armature has 600 turns, and 3% of torque is lost to friction. Calculate the developed power.

## Solution

Total armature conductors:

\[
Z=2\times\text{number of turns}=2(600)=1200
\]

For lap winding:

\[
A=P=6
\]

Induced back emf:

\[
E_b=\frac{\Phi ZPN}{60A}
=\frac{0.08(1200)(6)(350)}{60(6)}
=560\,\text{V}
\]

Taking the stated 400 A as armature current:

\[
P_{dev}=E_bI_a=560(400)=224\,\text{kW}
\]

This is the gross mechanical power developed in the armature.

If 3% of torque is lost to friction, the speed is unchanged at the same operating point, so shaft output power is also reduced by 3%:

\[
P_{shaft}=0.97P_{dev}=0.97(224)=217.28\,\text{kW}
\]

> Check: the phrase "total developed power" normally means \(P_{dev}=224\,\text{kW}\), for which the friction statement is not needed. The shaft output after the stated friction loss is also given to cover the likely intended question.
