# DC Shunt Motor Back-EMF Calculation

## Problem

A 250 V DC shunt motor takes 30 A at full load. The armature and field resistances are \(0.1\,\Omega\) and \(200\,\Omega\), respectively. Determine the back emf at full load.

## Solution

Given:

\[
V=250\,\text{V}, \quad I_L=30\,\text{A}, \quad R_a=0.1\,\Omega, \quad R_f=200\,\Omega
\]

Field current:

\[
I_f=\frac{V}{R_f}=\frac{250}{200}=1.25\,\text{A}
\]

Armature current:

\[
I_a=I_L-I_f=30-1.25=28.75\,\text{A}
\]

Back emf:

\[
E_b=V-I_aR_a
=250-28.75(0.1)
=247.125\,\text{V}
\]

Therefore, the back emf is \(\boxed{247.125\,\text{V}}\).
