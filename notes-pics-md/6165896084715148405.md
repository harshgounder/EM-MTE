# Three-Phase Transformer, Study Prompts

This image contains short revision prompts rather than worked notes.

## Connections

The page notes a delta-to-star connection, written as \(\Delta\)-Y, and the delta relation:

\[
V_L=V_{ph}\qquad\text{for a delta connection}
\]

For a star connection:

\[
V_L=\sqrt{3}\,V_{ph}
\]

> Check: a \(\Delta\)-Y connection is not automatically step-up. Whether it steps voltage up or down is determined by the turns ratio as well as the chosen primary and secondary sides.
