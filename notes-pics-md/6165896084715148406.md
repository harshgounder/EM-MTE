# DC Machines, Generator Fundamentals

## Types of DC generator

1. Separately excited generator.
2. Shunt generator.
3. Series generator.
4. Compound generator:
   - Cumulative compound generator.
   - Differential compound generator.

## Conditions for voltage build-up in a self-excited shunt generator

The handwritten list is labelled "separately excited," but these conditions apply to a **self-excited shunt generator**:

1. Residual magnetism must be present.
2. Field-winding connection must reinforce the residual flux.
3. Speed must exceed the critical speed for the field resistance.
4. Field-circuit resistance must be below the critical field resistance for the operating speed.

## Worked problem

A 4-pole DC machine runs at 1800 rpm. Its armature has 90 slots with 6 conductors per slot. Flux per pole is \(6\times10^{-2}\,\text{Wb}\). The coils are lap connected. If current per conductor is 100 A, determine induced emf and electrical output power.

### Given

\[
P=4, \quad N=1800\,\text{rpm}, \quad \Phi=0.06\,\text{Wb}
\]

\[
Z=90\times6=540\ \text{conductors}
\]

For a lap winding, \(A=P=4\) parallel paths.

### Induced emf

\[
E_g=\frac{\Phi ZPN}{60A}
\]

\[
E_g=\frac{0.06(540)(4)(1800)}{60(4)}=972\ \text{V}
\]

### Electrical output power

Each parallel path carries the conductor current, so:

\[
I_a=A(100)=4(100)=400\ \text{A}
\]

Neglecting losses:

\[
P_{elec}=E_gI_a=972(400)=388.8\ \text{kW}
\]
