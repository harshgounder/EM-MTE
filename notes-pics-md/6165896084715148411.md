# DC Series Motor Applications and Speed Control

## Applications of a DC series motor

A DC series motor is used where high starting torque is required, for example:

- Electric traction and locomotives.
- Cranes and hoists.
- Starter motors.

The series field carries armature current, so it uses thick wire with comparatively few turns. Its resistance is low:

\[
R=\rho\frac{L}{A}
\]

## Speed equation

\[
E_b=\frac{\Phi ZPN}{60A}
\]

Rearranging:

\[
N\propto\frac{E_b}{\Phi}
\]

Since \(E_b=V-I_aR_a\):

\[
N\propto\frac{V-I_aR_a}{\Phi}
\]

## Speed-control methods

### Field, or flux, control

Weakening the field reduces \(\Phi\) and increases speed. Increasing the flux decreases speed.

### Armature control

Two ways to reduce speed are:

1. Add resistance in series with the armature, which increases voltage drop.
2. Reduce the armature supply voltage.

> Check: the source says "field control, increase the speed." This is true only when the control weakens the field flux.
