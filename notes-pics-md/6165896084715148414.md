# DC Shunt-Motor Speed, Worked Example, Part 2

This page completes the 250 V shunt-motor problem from the preceding image.

## Loaded condition

\[
I_{L2}=50\,\text{A}
\]

The shunt-field current remains constant:

\[
I_f=1\,\text{A}
\]

\[
I_{a2}=I_{L2}-I_f=50-1=49\,\text{A}
\]

\[
E_{b2}=V-I_{a2}R_a
=250-49(0.2)
=240.2\,\text{V}
\]

At constant flux, back emf is proportional to speed:

\[
\frac{E_{b1}}{E_{b2}}=\frac{N_1}{N_2}
\]

\[
N_2=\frac{E_{b2}N_1}{E_{b1}}
=\frac{240.2(1000)}{248.6}
=966.2\,\text{rpm}
\]

Therefore, the loaded speed is \(\boxed{966.2\,\text{rpm}}\).
