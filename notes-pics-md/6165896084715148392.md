# Secondary Induced Voltage, Continuation

This page completes part 2 of the transformer problem on the preceding image.

For an ideal transformer:

\[
\frac{E_1}{E_2}=\frac{N_1}{N_2}
\]

Therefore:

\[
E_2=E_1\frac{N_2}{N_1}
=400\frac{1080}{380}
=1136.84\ \text{V}
\]

> Check: the source writes \(E_2=1200\,\text{V}\). That result does not follow from its own substitution, \(400\times1080/380\). The correct result is approximately \(1137\,\text{V}\).
