# DC-Machine Revision List and Induction-Motor Basics

## Fleming's rules

- **Right-hand rule**: generator action, induced-current direction.
- **Left-hand rule**: motor action, force or motion direction.

- Reversal and starting of a DC motor.

## Losses and reduction methods

- Copper loss is generally largest in the armature circuit because armature current is high.
- Iron loss consists of:
  - Eddy-current loss, reduced by laminating the core.
  - Hysteresis loss, reduced by using silicon steel.

## Induction motor

Useful features listed on the page: rugged construction, self-starting operation, low operating cost, and good efficiency.

### Synchronous speed

\[
N_s=\frac{120f}{P}
\]

where \(f\) is supply frequency in hertz and \(P\) is the number of poles.

For 50 Hz supply:

\[
N_s\text{ for 2 poles}=\frac{120(50)}2=3000\,\text{rpm}
\]

\[
N_s\text{ for 4 poles}=\frac{120(50)}4=1500\,\text{rpm}
\]

> Check: the source writes 1800 rpm for the 50 Hz, 4-pole example. The correct value is 1500 rpm. A 60 Hz, 4-pole motor has 1800 rpm synchronous speed.

### Slip

\[
\text{slip speed}=N_s-N_r
\]

\[
s=\frac{N_s-N_r}{N_s}
\]

\[
N_r=(1-s)N_s
\]

where \(N_r\) is rotor speed and \(s\) is per-unit slip.
