The Induce Torque Equations Of Real Machines

**Left Column Text:**
The torque in any dc machine depends on three factors:
*   The flux $\Phi$ in the machine
*   The armature (or rotor) current $I_A$ in the machine
*   A constant depending on the construction of the machine

$\tau_{ind} = \frac{ZP}{2\pi a} \Phi I_A = K \Phi I_A$
$K = \frac{ZP}{2\pi a}$

$Z = 2CN_c$

**Right Column Formula Box 3 (Bottom):**
$a = 2m \text{ for wave winding}$
$a = mP \text{ for lap winding}$
