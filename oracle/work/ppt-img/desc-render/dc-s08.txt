**EMF Equation of DC Generator**

Let:

*   $P$ = number of poles
*   $\Phi$ = flux produced by each pole in weber (Wb)
*   $Z$ = total number of armature conductors
*   $N$ = armature speed in rpm
*   $A$ = number of parallel paths

Total flux produced by all poles:

$P\Phi$

During **one revolution**, each armature conductor cuts the flux from all $P$ poles. Therefore, flux cut by one conductor per revolution is:

$\boxed{P\Phi}$

If the armature rotates at $N$ rpm, then the time required for one revolution is:

$t = \frac{60}{N} \text{ seconds}$
