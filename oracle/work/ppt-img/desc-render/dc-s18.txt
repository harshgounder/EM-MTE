### (2) Visual Elements
The slide contains an **equivalent circuit diagram for a shunt DC generator**, located on the lower-right portion of the slide:
1.  Components:
    - Armature voltage source: a circle with `+` (top) and `-` (bottom) labels, marked $E_A$
    - Armature resistance: a zigzag resistor symbol, marked $R_A$, in series with $E_A$
    - Parallel shunt field branch: a series combination of an adjustable (variable) zigzag resistor marked $R_F$ (with an arrow indicating adjustability) and an inductor coil symbol marked $L_F$, connected directly across the generator's output terminals (shunt connection)
    - Output terminals: open terminals on the right, with terminal voltage $V_T$ labeled between them (`+` on top terminal, `-` on bottom terminal)
2.  Current labels with arrows:
    - $I_A$: arrow pointing right, indicating armature current flowing through $R_A$ toward the central node
    - $I_F$: arrow pointing down, indicating field current flowing into the parallel shunt branch ($R_F$ and $L_F$)
    - $I_L$: arrow pointing right, indicating load current flowing from the central node to the positive output terminal
3.  Below the circuit, three equations are printed (transcribed in full above).
