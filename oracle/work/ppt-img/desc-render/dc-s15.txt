**Header:**

**Title:**
The Separately Excited Generator

**Main Text (Bullet Points):**
*   A separately excited dc generator is a generator whose field current is supplied by a separate external dc voltage source.
*   By Kirchhoff's voltage law, the terminal      voltage
    is          $V_T = E_A - I_A R_A$
*   Since the internal generated voltage is independent of IA the terminal characteristic of the separately excited generator is a straight line

**Circuit Diagram Labels:**
*   Left circuit (Field): $V_F$ (with + and - signs), $I_F$, $R_F$ (variable resistor symbol), $L_F$
*   Right circuit (Armature): $I_A$, $R_A$, $E_A$ (with + and - signs), $I_L$, $V_T$ (with + and - signs)
*   Equations below the circuits:
    *   $I_L = I_A$
    *   $V_T = E_A - I_A R_A$
    *   $I_F = \frac{V_F}{R_F}$
*   Caption below circuits: A separately excited dc generator

**Graphs (Bottom Right):**
*   **Graph (a):** Vertical axis label: $V_T$, $E_A$. Horizontal axis label: $I_L$. Annotations: $I_A R_A$ drop (indicated by a bracket). Bottom label: (a)
*   **Graph (b):** Vertical axis label: $V_T$, $E_{A nf}$. Horizontal axis label: $I_L$. Annotations: $I_A R_A$ drop, AR drop (indicated by brackets). Bottom label: (b)

*   **Header Bar:** Contains a university logo (left) and a NAAC accreditation seal (right).
*   **Circuit Diagrams:** Located at the top right. 
    *   The **field circuit** (left of the two) shows an external DC voltage source ($V_F$), a variable resistor ($R_F$), and an inductor ($L_F$) in series, representing the field winding. 
    *   The **armature circuit** (right of the two) shows a generated EMF source ($E_A$) in series with an armature resistance ($R_A$), leading to output terminals where terminal voltage ($V_T$) and load current ($I_L$) are measured.
*   **Graphs:** Two vertical plots at the bottom right.
    *   **Graph (a)** shows a linear drop in terminal voltage ($V_T$) as load current ($I_L$) increases. The difference between the constant internal voltage ($E_A$) and the terminal voltage is labeled as the resistive drop ($I_A R_A$ drop).
    *   **Graph (b)** shows a slightly more pronounced drop. It includes the resistive drop ($I_A R_A$ drop) and an additional drop labeled "AR drop" (Armature Reaction drop), indicating how flux distortion affects the voltage when compensating windings are absent.
