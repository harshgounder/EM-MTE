### 2. Visual Element Description
The slide features a series of five technical diagrams illustrating the effect of **Armature Reaction** in a DC machine.

*   **Diagram (a):** Shows the main magnetic field from two poles (N on the left, S on the right) acting on a circular armature. A vertical line represents the **Magnetic neutral plane**. A curved arrow labeled **$\omega$** at the top indicates counter-clockwise rotation. Conductors are represented by small circles: dots (current coming out) on the left half and crosses (current going in) on the right half.
*   **Diagram (b):** Shows only the magnetic field produced by the armature current (**Armature field**), with flux lines forming a horizontal pattern passing through the armature.
*   **Diagram (c):** Shows the **resultant magnetic field**, which is a combination of the pole field and armature field. The flux lines are distorted and the density is reduced under the poles (weakening the flux).
*   **Diagram (d):** Shows the two fields superimposed, specifically labeling the **Pole field** (vertical flux between poles) and the **Armature field** (horizontal flux).
*   **Diagram (e):** Shows the shift in the neutral plane. The **Old neutral plane** (vertical) is compared to the **New neutral plane**, which has rotated in the direction of rotation.
